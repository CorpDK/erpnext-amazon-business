# Amazon Business — user guide (stub)

P0 ships schema and master data only. End-user flows (Connect storefronts, sync purchases, Create Purchase Invoice, reconciliation) are documented here as **P1** lands.

## What you can do today

1. Install the app on a Frappe v16 site with ERPNext.
2. Open **Amazon Business** workspace.
3. Review **Amazon Business Settings** (connection mode, phase flags).
4. Create **Amazon Business Company Config** per ERP Company and enable storefront rows (connections activate in P1).

## Coming in P1

- OAuth (Direct or Relay) per storefront
- Scheduled and manual sync into **Amazon Business Purchase**
- **Create Purchase Invoice** and reconciliation desk tool

Refer to CorpDK/ERP design docs for the full product specification.
